### 1. Intuitive Drag-and-Drop Editor
This is the foundational feature for any "non-designer" friendly tool. The ability to simply click, drag, and drop elements onto a canvas is paramount. I expect ClickDesigns X to offer a highly responsive and fluid editor that allows users to:
*   **Manipulate elements:** Easily resize, rotate, group, and align text, images, shapes, and other design components.
*   **Layer management:** Work with multiple layers to create complex designs, bringing elements forward or sending them backward effortlessly.
*   **Undo/Redo functionality:** Essential for experimentation and correcting mistakes without frustration.
*   **Precision controls:** While drag-and-drop is key, I anticipate options for precise positioning using numerical inputs or alignment guides for pixel-perfect designs.

*Real-world application:* Imagine needing to quickly adjust a headline on an ad banner. Instead of fiddling with complex text boxes in traditional software, you simply click the text, drag it to a new position, and resize it with a quick pull of the corner. This speed is crucial for agile marketing.
