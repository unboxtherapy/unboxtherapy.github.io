## How Does Self Publish Pilot Work?

The beauty of Self Publish Pilot lies in its simplicity, truly adhering to the "Highlight. Click. Publish." mantra. Based on my review and the detailed demo, the workflow is incredibly intuitive, making it accessible even for those who might be new to AI tools or KDP publishing.

### Step-by-Step Workflow Explanation

1.  **Installation:** The first step, naturally, is to install the Self Publish Pilot browser extension. This is a standard process, usually taking mere seconds from the Chrome Web Store (or equivalent for other browsers if supported, though the sales page emphasizes "browser-based" without specifying beyond Chrome). Once installed, a small icon appears in your browser's toolbar.

2.  **Browse & Discover:** This is where the magic begins. You simply browse the internet as you normally would. This could be:
    *   An Amazon bestseller page for competitor analysis.
    *   A review section of a product or book.
    *   A popular blog post in a niche you're considering.
    *   An article on a trending topic.
    *   A forum discussion where people are airing their problems.

3.  **Highlight Relevant Text:** Once you find a piece of text that sparks an idea or contains valuable information, you simply highlight it with your mouse, just like you would to copy text. This highlighted text serves as the contextual input for the AI.

4.  **Click the Extension Icon:** After highlighting, you click the Self Publish Pilot extension icon in your browser toolbar. A small menu or sidebar will then pop up, presenting you with a list of the 12 (or more, depending on updates or OTOs) available AI workflows.

5.  **Choose Your Workflow:** From the presented options, you select the specific output you want to generate. For instance, if you highlighted a competitor's book description, you might choose "Competitor Book Breakdown" or "Title & Subtitle Ideas." If you highlighted a blog post about a problem, you might select "Book Idea Angles" or "Reader Pain Points."

6.  **Instant Generation:** Within seconds – sometimes even faster – the AI processes the highlighted text and generates the requested output. This output typically appears directly within the extension's interface, ready for you to review, copy, and use.
