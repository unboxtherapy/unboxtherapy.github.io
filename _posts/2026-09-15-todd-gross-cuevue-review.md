## What is CueVue?

At its heart, CueVue is a groundbreaking web engagement platform designed to bring personalized, real-time interaction to any website. Imagine your website not just *having* answers, but *giving* answers back, instantly and conversationally. That's the essence of CueVue. It integrates a "lifelike LiveRep™" – an AI-driven avatar – directly onto your existing website. This LiveRep™ acts as a virtual representative, capable of listening to visitor questions, processing them against an approved knowledge base, and responding in real-time.

The product fits squarely into the burgeoning niche of conversational AI and intelligent virtual assistants, but with a distinct twist: its emphasis on a "lifelike" video avatar and its proactive approach to guiding visitors. Unlike traditional text-based chatbots that can often feel clunky or impersonal, CueVue aims for a more engaging, human-like interaction that mirrors a genuine conversation.

The primary purpose of CueVue is clear: to enhance visitor engagement, clarify information, and, most importantly, guide visitors towards a "useful next step." This could be anything from making an enquiry, booking an appointment, choosing a product, or signing up for a service. The sales page highlights this crucial benefit: "Your page has the answer. Now it can answer back. In real time."

CueVue is built for a broad target audience. I see it as incredibly valuable for small to medium-sized businesses, local service providers, e-commerce stores, coaches, consultants, digital agencies, and anyone who relies on their website to generate leads, support customers, or drive sales.
